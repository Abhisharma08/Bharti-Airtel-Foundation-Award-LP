About the awards
07 · About the awards

Recognising India’s Finest Educators

Every day, exceptional teachers are shaping lives, inspiring students, and creating meaningful change in classrooms and communities across India. If you are one of them, this is your opportunity to gain national recognition for your impact. If you know a teacher whose work deserves to be celebrated, nominate them today. The Bharti Airtel Foundation Teacher Excellence Awards honour educators who are transforming learning and helping shape the future of India.
