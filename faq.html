Teacher Excellence Awards 2027 / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about applying for the Bharti Airtel Foundation Teacher Excellence Awards.

What are the Bharti Airtel Foundation Teachers Excellence Awards 2026?

The Bharti Airtel Foundation Teacher Excellence Awards 2026 aim to recognize teachers across India who have demonstrated lasting, evidence-based innovation and impact on student learning and development. The Awards seek to identify, celebrate and amplify innovative and impactful teaching practices at the regional and national levels.

What is the Award structure?

The Awards follow a Regional and National structure. Applicants will first be evaluated within their respective region: North, South, East or West, with selected applicants progressing to the National Level.

Are there different award categories?

No. The Teacher Excellence Awards do not have separate award categories or segments. All eligible applicants will be evaluated under a common evaluation framework.

How will the Regional and National Winners be selected?

Applicants will first undergo evaluation at the Regional Level, including assessment by a Regional Jury. Selected applicants from each region will then progress to the National Level, where they will be evaluated by the National Jury.

At the conclusion of the selection process, one Regional Level Winner will be recognised from each of the four regions: North, South, East and West, along with one National Level Winner.

Can I submit my application in any language?

Yes. The application form can be completed and submitted in English or any of the other languages available on the Bharti Airtel Foundation Teacher Excellence Awards Portal. Note: Choice of language will not affect the evaluation process

Who is eligible to participate in the Awards?
  • The applicant must be currently employed as a full-time teacher at a recognized government, government-aided, local body, or private school in India.
  • The applicant must have a minimum of four years of aggregate full-time teaching experience as of 30 June 2026; the experience need not have been at the applicant's current school.
  • The initiative, practice, or intervention submitted for the Award should have been implemented or undertaken during the period July 01, 2024 to June 30, 2026 (or a major part of the initiative, practice, or intervention should fall within the defined period), with impact demonstrated and results showcased during the period July 01, 2024 to June 30, 2026.
  • The submitted work may involve collaboration with other teachers, school leadership, parents, community members, organisations or other stakeholders. However, the applicant's personal role, contribution and leadership must be clearly identifiable and material to the outcomes being presented.
  • The Awards shall have Pan-India coverage, with applicants classified into the following four regions:
    • North: Chandigarh, Delhi, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh.
    • South: Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Lakshadweep, Puducherry, Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
    • East: Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Jharkhand, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Sikkim, Tripura, West Bengal.
    • West: Gujarat, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Goa, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu.
  • The applicant must not be solely engaged in private tuition or employed as a faculty member of a college or university.
How do I apply for the Awards?
  • Eligible applicants can apply through the Bharti Airtel Foundation Teacher Excellence Awards Portal, accessible through TheTeacherApp.
  • Applicants will need to register on the Portal, following which the application form can be accessed and completed online. The completed application, along with the required supporting documents, must be submitted through the Portal within the specified timeline.
  • Applicants are advised to review the eligibility criteria and Terms and Conditions before submitting their application.
Can I submit multiple applications under the Awards?

No. Each applicant may submit only one application for the Teacher Excellence Awards. Multiple applications from the same applicant will not be considered.

Is it mandatory to provide supporting documents?
  • Yes. Applicants are required to provide relevant supporting documents or evidence to substantiate the information and achievements presented in their application.
  • Applicants may either upload the supporting documents directly on the Awards Portal or provide accessible drive links, wherever permitted. All uploaded files must comply with the formats and file-size limits specified on the Portal.
What evidence or supporting documents are required to be submitted with the application?

Applicants are required to submit the following mandatory evidence:

  • School Head/Principal Endorsement or NOC
  • Evidence of Student/Learner Impact

Applicants may also submit optional supporting evidence to strengthen their application, such as:

  • Photographs, videos, or relevant links
  • Testimonials from students, parents, colleagues, or beneficiaries
  • Evidence of recognition, adoption, or replication of the initiative/practice
  • Teacher-created materials, such as lesson plans or toolkits
  • Reference contacts who can provide additional information or validate the work
What happens to my application once it is submitted?
  • Applications will be reviewed for completeness, eligibility and adherence to the application requirements. Incomplete or ineligible applications will not be taken forward.
  • Eligible applications will undergo further evaluation based on the defined evaluation criteria.
  • Shortlisted applicants will be informed about the subsequent stages of the selection process, as applicable.
Is there an application fee associated with the Awards?

There is no application fee to apply or participate in the Awards.

Can I make changes to my application after it has been submitted?

No. Once submitted, changes cannot be made to the application. Applicants are advised to carefully review all information and supporting documents before final submission.

Can I complete the application offline and submit it later?

No. Applications must be completed and submitted online through the Awards Portal. However, you can use the Save as Draft option to save your progress and return to complete the application before final submission.

Can I save my application and complete it later?

Yes. You can save your application as a draft and return to complete it before the submission deadline. Please ensure that the application is finally submitted within the specified timeline.

What is the deadline for submission of entries?

Completed applications must be submitted by the first week of January 2027.

When will the results be announced?

The results will be announced at the Awards Ceremony, scheduled to be held in May 2027.

How many winners will be recognised?

The Awards will recognise one National Level Winner and four Regional Level Winners, one each from the North, South, East and West regions.

How will I know if I have been shortlisted?

Shortlisted applicants will be contacted directly by the Awards Management using the contact details provided in their application. Any additional information or documentation required at subsequent stages will also be communicated accordingly.

Where can I access the Terms & Conditions for the Awards?

The Terms & Conditions will be available on the Awards Website/Portal. Applicants are advised to review them carefully before submitting their application.

Who can I contact if I face any issues while applying?

For any queries or assistance related to the application process, please write to us at TeacherAwards@bhartiairtelfoundation.org.

Can someone nominate a teacher for the Awards?

Yes. Teachers may apply themselves or be nominated by someone who wishes to recognize their contribution.

What will the winners receive?
  • The National Level Winner will receive a cash prize of ₹10 lakh, while each of the four Regional Level Winners will receive ₹5 lakh, subject to applicable taxes, statutory deductions and T&Cs.
  • Winners will be offered an all-expense-paid exposure visit to Bhutan to learn about the Bhutan Baccalaureate.
  • Winners will have opportunities for continued engagement, including publication of a white paper, engagement on TheTeacherApp, mentoring other teachers, and hosting webinars on TheTeacherApp YouTube channel.
